This is the version where MM-Companion stops being a sheet you read and becomes a sheet you play from. You can roll any stat straight off the character sheet — and an attack now hands the save it forces to the player who has to make it. The dice roller lives on the sheet itself, pinned beside your character so it never scrolls away. Sessions can live on a public server instead of the GM's laptop. And the whole app can be re-dressed: pick a theme, edit any colour, size or piece of artwork in it, and save your own.

## What's new

### Roll straight from the sheet

Double-click any ability, resistance, skill or your Initiative and the roller picks it up with the right number already in — including any penalties your current conditions impose, so what you roll always matches what the sheet shows. A power card's dice footer gets a 🎲 beside every line the wielder rolls. The loaded trait sticks around after the throw, so you can nudge the bonus and roll again.

Attacks now chain. A hit posts its own result plus a button for the save it forces — "🎲 Toughness vs. 18" — and that button appears on *everyone's* copy of the roll, so the target's player clicks it straight out of the shared history, with their own resistance already filled in. Criticals are handled: a natural 20 raises the save's DC, and a natural 1 that still hits gives the target a bonus, with the reason written on the chip so a changed number explains itself. A resolved save states what actually happened to you — dazed, staggered, incapacitated — read from the effect's own outcome ladder.

### The dice roller is part of the sheet

There is no separate roller window any more. The roller is a block like any other, and it starts **pinned** to a strip beside the page — a strip that does not scroll, so your dice stay in view while you scroll through your powers. You can drag the strip to any edge of the window, put two blocks side by side in it, and pin or unpin any block from its title bar. The dice block reshapes itself to the space it's in: one tall column in a side strip, one wide row along the bottom.

Saved rolls got simpler. Click the ☆ on any roll in the history to keep it as a quick-roll chip — one click, no dialog — and click the ★ to drop it again. Rename a chip from its right-click menu.

### Sessions that live on a server

A session no longer depends on the GM's machine staying awake. Anyone who installs the app can host one on the public server — no account, no setup, and a fresh install already points at it. The join code works exactly as before; players can't tell the difference. The GM can then connect to their own session from anywhere and run it: player sheets, hidden rolls, kicking and renaming seats all work from the remote seat, and the table survives the GM closing their laptop.

### Themes, and a Settings window to edit them

Preferences now open a proper Settings window, with Themes as its first page. Four presets ship — the original look plus Slate Dark, Parchment Light and Crimson Gold — and any of them can be duplicated into one of your own. The editor lists *every* colour, size, spacing value, font size and piece of artwork the app uses, with a search box to find one, and previews each change on the running app as you make it. Hero points are now medallions rather than plain circles, and a theme picks which die and which medallion artwork it draws.

## Changes since 0.3.0

- **Added** rolling from the sheet — abilities, resistances, skills, initiative and power lines — with condition penalties folded in.
- **Added** attack → forced-save chains, shared with the whole table, including critical hits and misses and per-effect outcome ladders.
- **Added** the pinned block strip: a non-scrolling strip on any edge of the window that any block can be pinned to.
- **Added** the dice roller as a sheet block, pinned there by default, that reflows to the space it's given.
- **Added** server-hosted sessions — host on the public server without an account, and run a session you aren't hosting.
- **Added** the Settings window with a full theme editor, four presets, saving your own themes, and selectable die and hero-point artwork.
- **Added** GM Mode NPCs: live NPC cards with initiative sorting, drag ordering, one-click conditions, duplication, a hover summary, and "Remove player" to kick a seat.
- **Added** a list of previous sessions to re-run or rejoin.
- **Changed** hero points to medallion pips that toggle individually, and every spend or gain is now written into the roll history.
- **Changed** the Power Constructor to explain itself, with the missing game terms filled in and real speeds on the movement modes.
- **Fixed** a batch of GM session bugs, and several cases where the sheet or the constructor could corrupt a build or show stale numbers.
- **Fixed** the theme presets missing from the installer, block captions forcing a block too wide, and assorted layout and visual polish.

## What's next

- More GM Mode: deeper encounter tools and session management.
- Equipment and devices — gear, equipment points, and device-based powers on the sheet.
- Printing and export, so you can take a sheet to the table on paper.
