An update about the things you do most at the table: rolling, and getting a
creature onto the board. Setting up a roll now takes one click instead of a
throw you didn't want, a power's roll line is a target you can actually hit
mid-fight, and a GM can conjure a usable NPC from five numbers and a name.

## Changes

- **Quick NPC wizard in GM mode.** Most NPCs a GM writes mid-session aren't
  builds — they're a name and four numbers. One page asks for what actually gets
  consulted (what it hits with, how hard it hits, and how hard it is to hit and
  to hurt) and hands back a creature that's saved, in the cast, and rollable
  against before its sheet even opens. It arrives with a damaging and a
  debilitating power at that rank; everything past that is edited on the ordinary
  NPC sheet, because it *is* an ordinary NPC. There's a name generator for when
  you don't have one ready.
- **One click loads a roll, two throw it.** The roller's chip only ever appeared
  *after* a roll, which is backwards — naming the trait is the first thing you
  do, and dialling in the situational bonus, penalty and DC is the second. Now a
  single click on an ability, resistance, skill or the Initiative readout puts it
  in the chip, and a double-click throws it. A closed Dice block reopens either
  way, so a click is never silently ignored.
- **A power's whole roll line is the button.** Hunting for a small die glyph is a
  poor target mid-fight, so the line itself is now what you click — outlined so
  it reads as clickable before you hover, and lit when the pointer's on it. Lines
  your *target* rolls stay inert, and the click falls through to the card, which
  still switches the power on and off.
- **Abilities and Resistances are proper tables.** They were hand-built grids
  sitting beside a Skills block that has always been a real table, and it showed
  — nothing said what the columns were. Both now carry Trait / ABL / Rank / Total
  headers and line up with each other, and the rank box no longer has to be
  locked before a double-click will roll the row.
- **Fixed washed-out dialogs on the system theme.** Message boxes and other
  pop-ups could come out with unreadable contrast when a system preset was
  active; they now follow the real OS palette.
- **Fixed the Dice block resizing when you join a session.** It keeps its height
  now, instead of jumping as the shared history takes over from the private one.
