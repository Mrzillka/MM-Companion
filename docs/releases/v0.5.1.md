A big update for a small version number. Two things you've probably wanted since
the sheet existed arrive together — undo, and somewhere to write things down —
and being a giant or a mouse is now a real, dial-able state rather than a number
you applied by hand. Plus a way for anyone at the table to ask everyone else for
a roll.

## Changes

- **Undo and redo on the character sheet.** Ctrl+Z walks the sheet back,
  Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y walks it forward, and two buttons sit beside the lock on
  the menu bar. It covers every block, a typed word counts as one step rather
  than ten, and each window keeps its own history.
- **A Notes block.** Markdown notes live on the sheet now, written in place with
  a toggle to see them rendered. Drop one Notes block onto another and they
  merge instead of fighting over the spot.
- **Growth and Shrinking are a ladder you climb.** A size power is a row of rungs
  you dial to the size you're actually at, not one leap to your full rank — click
  the lit rung to switch it off again. Being that size now modifies the character
  properly: your reach, your Strength and Toughness, how hard you are to notice,
  and your damage all follow the size you're at.
- **Speeds add up.** Every source of movement stacks instead of the biggest one
  winning, with one line per way of moving — walking, flight, swimming, burrowing
  — so the first ranks of Speed you buy finally do something, and a jet's flight
  shows on its own line rather than swallowing your legs.
- **Ask the table for a roll.** The roller has a Request row: pick a trait, set a
  DC, press Ask, and everyone gets a button that rolls it on their own character.
  No attack needed — "everyone roll Perception vs DC 15" is now a thing you can
  say.
- **The GM's roller shape is available to everyone.** It's a preference now —
  Normal, Compact or Extended — instead of a layout only GM Mode could have.
- **NPC cards collapse**, and you can walk a creature down the damage ladder
  straight from its card.
- **A power remembers what it was set to.** Switched on, held at a size rung,
  which member of an array is live — all of it survives saving and reopening the
  file. It costs nothing extra; dialling a power down still refunds no points.
- **A power group says which mode it's in**, so you can tell an array from a set
  of separate powers at a glance.
- Fixed the damage ladder putting conditions back after it removed them, and a
  dead creature still carrying the Dying and Incapacitated it passed through.
- Fixed a size power paying out twice — once in your walking pace and again in
  Power Level validation.
- Fixed the sheet's unsaved marker lying to you: undoing past your last save now
  stars the title and prompts before closing.
- Fixed the Notes block losing a note to a failed write, and a deleted note
  coming back from the dead.
- Fixed the roller mis-sizing itself in Compact, and a long roll name wrapping
  against the block's border.
- Fixed the lock, the pinned strip and a drag quietly resizing blocks behind your
  back, and a table's first column clipping a long name instead of wrapping it.
- Various visual polish across the four table blocks, the toolbars and the menu
  bar under themed presets, plus smaller fixes.
