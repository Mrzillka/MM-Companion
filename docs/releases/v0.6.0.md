The version about doing more than you paid for. Every power in the app used to be
exactly what you bought and nothing else — and that is not how a Mutants &
Masterminds hero wins a fight. This one adds the three things that bend a build at
the table: Extra Effort, power stunts, and a Dynamic array whose points you shove
around with your thumb between one attack and the next. Underneath it, the whole
powers layer has been read back against the core rulebook and corrected, so the
numbers a card prints are the numbers the book asks for.

## What's new

### Extra Effort and power stunts

Extra Effort is a real thing your character can spend now, from the System block
and from a power's own card. Push an effect's rank past what you bought, push
your Strength or one of your movement modes, take the +2 on a check — it lands in
the roller's bonus slider so you don't have to remember it — or buy back a use of
something. It ignores your Power Level limits on purpose, and it costs you a rung
of the fatigue ladder a turn later, applied for you. Power stunts are here too:
spend a Hero Point and a rung, build the temporary alternate effect in the
constructor, and it appears as its own card marked **Stunt** where every other
card prints a cost. It's checked against the ceiling the rules give it, it costs
nothing, and it vanishes at the end of the scene rather than being saved into your
character file.

### Dynamic arrays you split with a slider

Dynamic is no longer a checkbox that asked the same question as the strip beside
it — it's the fourth segment on the structure strip, next to Independent, Linked
and Array. Splitting the pool is a slider per member: drag it and the handle *is*
the share, with the member's name at each end and what those points currently buy
right there on the label, so you're choosing "Flight 4 of 5" rather than typing
points at each other. A slider ends where the pool does and gains a notch every
time a sibling hands one back, so every stop is legal for everyone at once.
Sliding a member to zero switches it off properly — a Growth dialled down to Off
now actually shrinks you back.

### Extended settings: partial modifiers, hard PL caps and rank dials

Three things the constructor's Extended settings page can now do. A modifier can
cover **only some ranks** of an effect — a Blast 12 whose top four ranks alone are
Tiring is buildable without paying for twelve. A power can be **held to Power
Level** as a hard cap rather than a warning marker: choose whether the effect rank
or the attack bonus gives way, and the card tells you what was shaved. And the
size ladder that Growth and Shrinking got last version is now a **rank dial any
effect can carry** — fire a Damage 10 at rank 5 and it really does force the
weaker save.

## Changes since 0.5.0

- **Added** Extra Effort — all six uses, the fatigue ladder walked for you, and
  the three advantages that bend it.
- **Added** power stunts: a temporary alternate effect bought with a Hero Point.
- **Added** Dynamic as a structure option at both levels an array exists at, with
  the pool split on the members' own sliders.
- **Added** rank bands, a hard Power Level cap and a general rank dial to the
  constructor's Extended settings.
- **Added** a proper Enhanced Trait picker — several traits at once, a skill's
  specialization (including one nobody has bought yet), advantages, and a price
  line you can read.
- **Added** the rulebook's standard power configurations, so a common build is
  something you pick rather than assemble.
- **Added** a menu of minions to a Summon with Variable Type, which is what that
  modifier actually buys.
- **Added** undo and redo on the character sheet, a Notes block for markdown notes,
  and the ability to ask the whole table for a roll.
- **Added** the Extended roller layout as a preference for everyone, not just GM
  Mode.
- **Changed** an array so only one of its effects runs at a time — the discount and
  the restriction are two halves of one rule, and only the discount was
  implemented. A **Using** picker on the card says which, and the others fade.
- **Changed** the Resistances block to three columns — Ability, Rank and Total —
  so you can see what made the number instead of one cell that hid it.
- **Changed** where the powers controls live: a modifier's rank band is edited in
  Extended settings as one list rather than on the chip, and the size strip of
  buttons is one slider.
- **Changed** Growth and Shrinking into a ladder of rungs you dial to the size
  you're at, with your reach, Strength, Toughness, stealth and damage all
  following it.
- **Changed** movement so every source of speed adds up, one line per way of
  moving, instead of the biggest one winning.
- **Changed** a power to remember what it was set to — switched on, held at a
  rung, which member of an array is live — across saving and reopening.
- **Fixed** the powers data against the core rulebook: a costing sweep over all
  302 modifier records, Removable priced from the whole power per 5 points,
  Concealment's senses, repeatability, the Dimensional sense, a Dynamic alternate
  effect charged what it costs, and an Affliction naming the effect its
  Transformed condition imposes.
- **Fixed** the sheet card's warning marker, which asked two questions where the
  constructor asked eight — an over-spent allocation or a broken build is now
  visible from the sheet.
- **Fixed** a sub-build not being held to its wielder's Power Level unless you
  opened it, and a multiselect charging twice for an option another one already
  covers.
- **Fixed** a power stunt being draggable into an array, where its cost of 0
  silently repriced the whole build.
- **Fixed** the share dial three ways: it switches its member off at zero, it sits
  where the member is actually running rather than on "Off", and it carries one
  notch per rank rather than per price, so no rung is unreachable.
- **Fixed** the size dial and the share dial deadlocking on the same card, and
  saving that deadlock to disk.
- **Fixed** a size power paying out twice, the damage ladder putting conditions
  back after removing them, the unsaved marker lying after an undo, and the Notes
  block losing a note to a failed write.
- Various visual polish across the powers cards, the table blocks and the themed
  presets, plus smaller fixes.

## What's next

- Conditions that carry their own dice — recovery checks and turn economy — and an
  initiative tracker.
- Groups of NPCs, for when the fight is against eight of something.
- A block editor, for arranging the sheet itself.
- Sample characters and a sample session, so the app can show what it does without
  you building something first.
- More mods, and more of the app opened up to them.
