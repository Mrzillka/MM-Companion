This update is about running a fight with other people in the room. The GM now
has a **Scene** — a shared board of who is in this fight, in what order, and what
state they are in — and every player watches it live on their own sheet. The app
is also open to mods that talk to the table, and the first one adds timers and
counters the GM can share.

## Changes

- **The Scene.** Drag a creature or a player onto the GM's Scene and the whole
  table sees it: the turn order, the conditions each one is under, and whether
  they are friend or foe. Roll initiative from a card's badge and the board sorts
  itself; right-click to clear a roll or take a creature off. Players get the same
  board pinned beside their dice, so a turn order never scrolls away mid-round.
- **Colour says friend or foe.** The GM marks each creature enemy, friendly or
  neutral and it is drawn on the card's edge, readable at a glance across a dozen
  of them. Your own entry is named as yours.
- **The board comes back.** Close the app mid-fight and the Scene is still there
  when you reopen it, with the initiatives you had rolled.
- **Mods can talk to the table.** A mod can now share live state with everyone at
  the session, put a block in the GM's window, and remember things between
  launches. Mods also install straight from a `.zip` now — **Manage Mods ▸ Add
  from Zip…** — instead of needing to be unpacked first.
- **Timers and counters**, as the first mod built on that: countdown dials and
  click-to-fill segment rings the GM sets up and shares one at a time. It is a
  separate download from
  [the mods repo](https://github.com/Mrzillka/mm-companion-mods), not bundled with
  the app.
- **Dynamic arrays behave.** A member's share dial has a notch per rank and
  switches the member off at zero; a share revives every switch the card put down;
  and a split array no longer claims one effect at a time.
- **Resistances show three numbers** — the ability, the ranks bought, and the
  total — rather than one, and the sheet reads out the Power Level limits a
  character is actually built to, plus the reach close attacks are made at.
- Various visual polish and smaller fixes across the powers constructor.
