The version about the sheet being yours. Every block on the character sheet used
to sit where the app put it, at the size the app chose — and a sheet is a thing
you read at a table, over hours, next to whatever else you have open. This one
turns the page into a grid you own: drag any block to any size and any place,
stack several into one tabbed cell, close the ones you never look at, and take
the whole gesture back with Ctrl+Z if you don't like it. Underneath it, the sheet
was made to keep up with you — editing a number no longer costs the app the best
part of a second.

## What's new

### A page you arrange yourself

The character sheet is a resizable grid now. Drag a divider and the blocks either
side of it give way; drag a block by its title bar and drop it above, below or
beside any other one, and the page re-deals itself around where you let go. Drop a
block *onto* another and the two share a cell with a tab bar, so Complications and
Conditions can live in the same square inch and you flip between them — the tabs
reorder by dragging, and a tab pulled out sideways becomes a cell of its own
again. Blocks close from their title bar and come back from the menu, pin to the
strip beside the page, or float off into their own window. Every one of those
moves is a step Ctrl+Z takes back, in with the edits you made to the character, in
the order you actually did them. Drag a block narrower and it adapts rather than
clipping — a table sheds columns, a row of panels stacks — down to the narrowest
arrangement it has, and the whole arrangement is remembered between launches.

### A sheet that keeps up

Editing a stat used to make the app rederive the entire build and rebuild every
power and equipment card, once per step — so dragging a spin box twenty-five
steps ran twenty-five full redraws and showed you the last one, a second later.
The build math is gathered once per pass now and the card trees redraw once a turn
rather than once a change, so a held arrow key moves the number as fast as you
hold it. A stat edit no longer flashes a window on screen either, which is the
other half of what made the sheet feel slow.

## Changes since 0.6.0

- **Added** a customisable page: resize by dragging dividers, move a block
  anywhere by its title bar, tab several into one cell, and close, pin or float
  any of them.
- **Added** layout undo — a divider drag, a move, a close and a tab click are all
  steps Ctrl+Z takes back, interleaved correctly with edits to the character.
- **Added** blocks that adapt to the width they are given, shedding columns and
  stacking panels instead of clipping, with the arrangement saved between
  launches.
- **Added** the Scene: a shared board of who is in the fight, in what order and
  under what conditions, that the GM builds by dragging creatures onto it and
  every player watches live on their own sheet.
- **Added** a session channel for mods — live state shared with the whole table, a
  block in the GM's window, and settings remembered between launches — plus
  installing a mod straight from a `.zip`.
- **Changed** a specialized skill pool to add its ranks to the skill it narrows:
  Stealth 10 with three ranks of Stealth: Hiding hides at 13, and the Skills
  block's "+" column says where the ranks came from.
- **Changed** an NPC's sheet to open with the roller, the Scene, Notes and
  Complications closed — they are in the way on a mook, and reopening one makes it
  stay open.
- **Changed** the Resistances block to three columns, and the sheet to read out the
  Power Level limits a character is built to and the reach close attacks are made
  at.
- **Fixed** Affliction offering three resistances where the rules name four, and
  Alternate Resistance opening to every resistance, ability and skill.
- **Fixed** Enhanced Trait: Improved Initiative moving no number, and "Use attack
  skill" never appearing for a character without a combat focus.
- **Fixed** several effects saying nothing the book says about them — Regeneration,
  Create, Morph, Quickness, Lifting, Move Object, Elongation and Teleport now print
  their own limits.
- **Fixed** reopening a minimized NPC sheet from its card, which did nothing at all.
- **Fixed** Dynamic arrays several ways: a share dial with a notch per rank that
  switches its member off at zero, a share reviving every switch the card put down,
  and a split array no longer claiming one effect at a time.
- Various visual polish and smaller fixes across the powers constructor and the
  sheet.

## What's next

- Conditions that carry their own dice — recovery checks and the turn economy that
  goes with them.
- Groups of NPCs, for when the fight is against eight of something.
- Sample characters and a sample session, so the app can show what it does without
  you building something first.
- More mods, and more of the app opened up to them.
- Powers and equipment cards that restate themselves rather than rebuilding, for
  the last of the redraw cost on a furnished sheet.
