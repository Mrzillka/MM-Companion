A follow-up to 0.7.0 that fixes the rough edges of online play. A GM whose
connection drops can now get the table back without restarting, rolls no longer
go missing from the shared history, and the roller stops quietly applying a
modifier you only meant to throw once.

## Changes

- **The GM can reconnect a dropped session.** If the link to the relay or the
  outside world goes down, the session no longer becomes a dead end that only a
  restart fixes — the window holds a notice with a *Reconnect now* button, and
  *Session ▸ Reconnect* is always there in the menu. Your join code stays the
  same, and everyone still at the table is left alone.
- **Your own rolls no longer vanish from the shared history.** A roll made
  around a reconnect, or one the table was slow to answer, could be shown to
  everybody else and stay invisible to you for the rest of the session. It now
  always turns up on your screen too.
- **A loaded roll is thrown once, not forever.** Double-clicking a stat used to
  leave the roller armed with it, so every later click of the die silently
  folded in that modifier and its difficulty. A chip loaded that way is now let
  go once its roll is made — loading it on purpose still sticks — and a loaded
  chip is much louder on screen, so the next throw is never a surprise.
- **Quick rolls are named.** A saved quick roll now reads "Strength" rather than
  "+4", and no longer carries a difficulty with it — the DC belongs to the
  situation in front of you, not to a roll you make often.
- **Increased Action and Increased Duration can be taken more than once.** As the
  rules allow: each rank moves the action or duration another step, instead of
  costing more and changing nothing.
- **More of the Dice block goes to the roll history.** Pinned beside the sheet or
  along the bottom, the roller no longer holds the window open wider than it
  needs or leaves a slab of empty space under the controls — the extra room goes
  to the history, which is the part that can use it.
