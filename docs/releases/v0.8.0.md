The version that keeps itself up to date. Until now, getting a new MM-Companion
meant noticing there was one, finding the release page, downloading the installer
and running it by hand. From 0.8.0 the app tells you when a newer version is out
and installs it for you, then opens again where you left off. It also rolls up
everything 0.7.1 fixed in online play, plus a handful of rules and roller fixes
of its own.

## What's new

### Updates from inside the app

The launcher now shows which version you are running in a small badge under its
buttons. When it opens, it checks quietly for a newer release, and if there is one
the badge says so and gets an **Update** button. Click it and the app downloads
the new installer, checks that it arrived intact, closes your sheets the normal
way (so anything unsaved still asks first), installs the update and starts again.
If Windows asks for permission and you say no, nothing changes and the app stays
open. If the install fails, the app still comes back and shows you what went
wrong. The startup check can be turned off in Settings, and the portable build,
which can't update itself in place, opens the download page instead.

This works from 0.8.0 onwards, so this is the last update you install by hand.

## Changes since 0.7.0

- **Added** in-app updates: the launcher shows your version, checks for a newer
  release, and installs it and restarts at one click.
- **Added** a way back for a GM whose session drops: the window shows a notice
  with a *Reconnect now* button, and *Session ▸ Reconnect* is always in the menu.
  The join code stays the same, and the players at the table stay connected.
- **Added** specialized combat skills to a power's attack skill list, so an
  attack can roll with a specialized skill such as Close Combat: Swords.
- **Changed** a roll loaded by double-clicking a stat so that it is used once,
  not for every roll after it. A chip you load on purpose still stays, and a
  loaded chip stands out much more on screen.
- **Changed** quick rolls to be saved under a name ("Strength" rather than "+4")
  and without a difficulty, since the DC depends on the moment, not the roll.
- **Changed** the Dice block to give its spare room to the roll history, whether
  it is pinned beside the sheet, along the bottom, or in the GM's window.
- **Fixed** your own rolls sometimes disappearing from the shared history for the
  rest of a session, while everyone else could still see them.
- **Fixed** Increased Action and Increased Duration so you can take them more than
  once, as the rules allow. Each rank moves the action or duration one more step.
- **Fixed** the switch to the compact roller stalling on a long roll history.
  The mini roller now also opens right where the full one was.
