An update for the table: a one-page simple sheet you can play from and print, a
batch of play-time conveniences, and a page layout that finally stays the way you
left it.

## Changes

- **Simple sheet.** View ▸ Simple Sheet shows your character on one tight page made
  for play — every roll, toggle and tracker still works there. Saved characters can
  open straight into it (Settings ▸ General).
- **Printing and PDF export.** File ▸ Print and File ▸ Export as PDF print the simple
  sheet as real, selectable text, and let you choose which blocks go on the page.
- **Advantage uses.** Per-adventure advantages such as Luck, Determination, Edit
  Scene and Well-Equipped get clickable dots on both sheets, and each use is written
  into the roll history.
- **Roll notifications.** New history entries pop up as a small always-on-top
  notification in a corner of the screen — switch it off or pick the corner in
  Settings ▸ General. Session notices now float over the window instead of pushing
  the blocks down, and hovering one holds it.
- **The roller's bonus and penalty are spent by the roll.** A circumstance bonus or
  penalty now applies to the one roll it was set for and then clears, and the
  sliders are highlighted while they hold anything — no more forgotten +2 riding
  along on every later roll.
- **Power ranks as pips.** A power card's rank slider is now a row of pips; click the
  last lit pip to step it down.
- **Skills.** An Untrained? column while editing, trained-only skills you cannot use
  are muted on the locked sheet, and Jack-of-All-Trades is honoured. Initiative is
  rollable like any other stat.
- **Blocks no longer resize on their own.** Unpinning, reopening or docking a block
  puts it back at the size it had, the side strip opens wide enough for the dice
  roller, and a new default layout pairs blocks into rows (Skills beside Advantages,
  Powers beside Equipment, Complications, Notes and Scene together). Your saved
  layout is untouched — View ▸ Reset Layout picks up the new default.
- Clicking a card no longer makes the whole sheet flicker and re-lay itself out.
- Denser roll-history cards, and various visual polish and smaller fixes.

**Install:** download `MM-Companion-Setup-0.8.1.exe` below and run it — or, if you
already have 0.8.0 installed, press **Update** on the launcher.
