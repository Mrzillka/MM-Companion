A fix for joining online sessions from Linux — the Steam Deck in particular — plus
quicker NPCs for the GM: build one from a Power Level and a preset, give it an icon,
and open it in a moment.

## Changes

- **Online play works on the Steam Deck and other Linux systems.** Joining a session
  through the relay could fail with "certificate verify failed: unable to get local
  issuer certificate" on SteamOS, Arch, Fedora and similar systems. The app now finds
  your system's trusted certificates on every Linux. The same fix lets the launcher
  see new versions there, which it could not before.
- **Quick NPC presets.** Pick a Power Level and a preset — Brute, Speed, Balance or
  Random — and Quick NPC fills in attack and effect, defence and toughness, and
  fortitude and will at the limits that level allows. You can still adjust any of
  them by hand. Your last Power Level and preset are remembered while the app is open,
  and pressing Enter creates the NPC.
- **NPC icons.** Choose one of eight built-in icons for an NPC instead of picking an
  image file.
- **NPCs open faster in GM Mode.** An NPC's sheet leaves out the blocks only a player
  needs, and its rolls go to the GM window's dice roller.
- Smaller fixes, including the compact button no longer covering the File menu on a
  sheet without a dice roller.

**Install:** on Windows, download `MM-Companion-Setup-0.8.3.exe` below and run it —
or, if you already have 0.8.2 installed, press **Update** on the launcher. On Linux,
download `MM-Companion-0.8.3-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, extract it and run `./install.sh`.
