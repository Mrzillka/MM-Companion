The version where your powers reach the other side of the table. Until now a roll
ended at a number, and the GM worked out by hand who it caught and what it did to
them. From 0.9.0 you can turn a check or an attack on several creatures in the scene
at once. The server rolls the whole group, each target's result lands in the history
as one table, and the conditions are applied automatically. This release also includes
everything from 0.8.1 to 0.8.3: the printable simple sheet, the Linux and Steam Deck
release, and quicker NPCs.

Everyone at the table needs 0.9.0 to join a session — older versions can no longer
connect. Press **Update** on the launcher.

## What's new

### Impress

A new **Impress** button in the roller's Action row lets you use an interaction
skill on the creatures in the scene to distract, demoralize or coerce them. Pick the impression and the skill separately (Demoralized with
Deception is fine) and tick one target or several; a group takes one check at the
group penalty. The GM always sets the situational modifiers and can add a bonus or
penalty against a single target. A targeted player gets a prompt to choose how to
resist. The server then rolls everything at once and applies the conditions each
failure earns. You can also send it as a request, in which case every target rolls
their own resistance from the history card. Impressions that need an advantage, such
as Dazed, Fascinated, Startled or Taunted, are shown greyed out with the advantage named, so you can see
what you're missing.

### Area attacks

You aim an area power by dragging scene cards into its window. You can name one
primary target, hit with an ordinary attack check at full rank; everyone else caught
in the area makes a Dodge check to take half. The GM resolves the NPCs in two
server-rolled steps, Dodge first and then the save, and the resulting conditions are
applied. Each player caught in the area gets a request to roll their Dodge and save
together. **Any attack can be used as an area at the table**: right-click the power
card and choose *Use as an area attack*. That rank is capped at your Power Level,
raised for larger sizes. The Area Effect extra now has a shape, extra applications
and a spread, and the power card states the area's size and Dodge DC. Every group
roll, Impress included, shows in the history as a single table.

### Table fixes from playtesting

- **Reroll.** A Reroll button throws your last roll again with a die that can't come
  up below 11, the way a Luck re-roll works. It uses the bonus and DC that roll was
  made with, a lifted 20 is not a critical, and in a session the server rolls it and
  marks it as a reroll for everyone. It spends nothing on its own, so mark the Hero
  Point or Luck rank yourself.
- **The GM can reveal a hidden roll.** A **Show** button on a hidden roll in the GM's
  history shows it to the whole table, including players who join later.
- **Alternate Resistance on any effect.** The extra now lets you choose which
  resistance the effect targets, plus a second one for "worst of" or "best of".
  A Damage effect resisted by Fortitude finally has somewhere to say Fortitude.
- The one-line descriptions of power effects were corrected — Extra Limbs grants no
  extra actions, Mind Reading only reads minds, Regeneration removes a Damage condition
  on a timer — and the vaguer ones now say what the effect does in play.

## Changes since 0.8.0

- **Added** Impress and area attacks (above), with every group roll shown as one
  table in the history.
- **Added** a Reroll button, a way for the GM to show a hidden roll to the table, and
  Alternate Resistance on any effect.
- **Added** the simple sheet: View ▸ Simple Sheet shows your character on one tight
  page made for play, with every roll, toggle and tracker still working. Saved
  characters can open straight into it (Settings ▸ General).
- **Added** printing and PDF export of the simple sheet as real, selectable text,
  with a choice of which blocks go on the page.
- **Added** a Linux release — a tarball with an install script, no Python or root
  needed — and a guide to running it on the Steam Deck, including from Game Mode.
- **Added** Quick NPC presets (Brute, Speed, Balance or Random at a chosen Power
  Level), eight built-in NPC icons, and NPC sheets that open faster in GM Mode.
- **Added** clickable uses for per-adventure advantages such as Luck and Edit Scene,
  pop-up roll notifications, and an Untrained? column on Skills.
- **Changed** the roller's circumstance bonus and penalty so they apply to one roll
  and then clear, and are highlighted while set.
- **Changed** a power card's rank slider to a row of pips.
- **Changed** blocks so they keep their size when unpinned, reopened or docked, and
  added a new default layout that pairs blocks into rows.
- **Fixed** joining online sessions from the Steam Deck and other Linux systems
  ("certificate verify failed").
- **Fixed** homebrew power overrides that showed one number and rolled another.
- **Fixed** the simple sheet's rows staying stacked after restoring a maximised
  window, and an Affliction's degrees printing too small on it.
- **Fixed** clicking a card making the whole sheet flicker and lay itself out again.
- Dev mode's build overrides now switch off along with Dev mode, plus various visual
  polish and smaller fixes.

**Install:** on Windows, download `MM-Companion-Setup-0.9.0.exe` below and run it —
or, if you already have 0.8.x installed, press **Update** on the launcher. On Linux,
download `MM-Companion-0.9.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, extract it and run `./install.sh`.
