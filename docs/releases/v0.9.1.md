A big update for the table. Impress and area attacks are reworked around the GM, NPC
cards are rebuilt and gain recovery checks, join codes are much shorter, and attacking
powers now roll and resist the way the book says they should.

**Everyone at an online table needs 0.9.1.** The public server is updated with this
release, and older versions can no longer join a session on it.

## Changes

- **Impress and area attacks, reworked.** A player's Impress now waits in the GM's
  history for the GM to resolve, like an area attack. Every roll that belongs to a
  player — their check, their Dodge, their save — is asked of them in a popup and a
  history card, and the result returns to the GM's window. The GM can start either
  action from an NPC card or for any player, adjust each check with one signed box,
  and run a linked area as one area per effect. A GM setting decides whether players
  set up their own actions or hand them to the GM.
- **The GM can answer rolls as an NPC.** Click a request or a save in the GM's history
  and pick an NPC from the scene, each listed with the number it would roll. A failed
  save puts its conditions on the NPC by itself (four GM settings switch this per
  kind), with a small undo button.
- **End turn** on a creature's card resists each impression on it again.
- **New NPC cards.** One cleaner header at two sizes, pinned numbers as a tidy grid,
  the creature's picture on the collapsed card, and names that wrap instead of being
  cut off. Every power can pin its effect rank, and new cards open expanded.
- **Recovery checks.** A die on each recoverable condition rolls the check to shake it
  off, for NPCs, for players (as a request) and on your own sheet.
- **Turn order on the scene.** Cards show their turn number, dragging one moves only
  that card, and disconnected players keep their seats.
- **Quick NPC** gains an Initiative field, and every preset now rolls Fortitude and
  Will.
- **Shorter join codes.** A code for the public server is now 24 characters instead of
  130. Old codes still work.
- **GM Mode reopens cleanly.** Closing the GM window ends that GM Mode, so reopening it
  starts fresh instead of showing a disconnected window. The start screen shows
  whether you're connected, with Connect, Disconnect and Reconnect buttons.
- **Attacking powers roll by the book.** Any power made into an attack gets its save
  and DC — a Teleport attack is resisted by Will at DC 10 + rank. A Linked power rolls
  its attack once and calls for every save its effects force. Roll lines now read
  "Attack +8 vs. Defense", and Healing, Mind Reading, Nullify and Move Object roll
  against the right DC or opposed check. The constructor warns about an Affliction or
  Resistible left without its choices, and about Linked effects with different
  bonuses.
- Mechanic descriptions in the action windows, Settings and the pickers now sit behind
  small "?" buttons, and roll cards in the history are narrower. Plus various visual
  polish and smaller fixes.

**Install:** on Windows, download `MM-Companion-Setup-0.9.1.exe` below and run it —
or, if you already have 0.9.0 installed, press **Update** on the launcher. On Linux,
download `MM-Companion-0.9.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, extract it and run `./install.sh`.
