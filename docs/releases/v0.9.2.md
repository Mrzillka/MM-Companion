A rules pass over every power effect and modifier. Effects that used to ask you for a
rank budget now cost what you pick, modifiers that only wrote a note now do what the
book says, and power cards spell out what each effect's rank actually means.

**Everyone at an online table needs 0.9.2.** The public server is updated with this
release, and older versions can no longer join a session on it.

## Changes

- **Effects priced by what you choose.** Enhanced Senses, Enhanced Movement,
  Comprehend, Concealment, Immunity and Feature no longer ask for a rank and then make
  you spend it: each choice is a row (what it is, its tier, and what it applies to),
  the same option can be taken twice — Accurate hearing beside Accurate smell — and the
  rank is what the rows add up to. Immunity offers the book's standard immunities with
  their ranks filled in. A character saved the old way shows a warning until its power
  is opened again.
- **Toughness defences work.** Impervious, Impenetrable, Hardened and Penetrating now
  decide what a Damage save does when the GM answers it for an NPC or resolves an area:
  an Impervious creature takes nothing from an attack it out-ranks, two degrees of
  success against a hardened Toughness leave no Hit, and Penetrating cuts back through.
  On your own sheet the save tells you to roll twice for Hardened.
- **Flaws that do something.** Tiring makes you Fatigued (then Exhausted) and
  Distracting makes you Vulnerable when you roll the power. Short-Term counts its six
  uses and Fades loses a rank per use, with Use once and Recharge on the card's menu.
  Unreliable rolls its reliability die, Leaping's Acrobatics flaw is a real check, and
  Check Required now adds your bonus to its roll.
- **Alternate Resistance on an Affliction** is picked on the extra itself, with every
  resistance, ability and skill to choose from, as on any other power.
- **Cards say what a rank means.** Communication and Remote Sensing show their range
  (same city, same continent, anywhere) instead of a distance; Environment shows the
  area it fills; Thrown attacks reach 2 + Strength. New lines show what Lifting lifts
  and throws, what Teleport carries and how far Extended takes it, Transmute's mass,
  Illusion's size, Obscure's area, Postcognition and Precognition uses, Extra Limbs,
  Mind Reading's levels of contact and more.
- **Modifier fixes from the book.** Quirk and the "per location / per use" extras are
  bought in ranks, Diminished Range actually shortens a range, Aura, Area Effect,
  Redirect and Triggered gain the priced options the rules give them, and every Limited,
  Noticeable and Sense-Dependent flaw has a field to say what it means. Immunity no
  longer shows a stray "Enhances" picker.
- **Linked attacks** roll at the first effect's bonus, and you can drag effect cards to
  reorder them in the Power Constructor.
- Fixed the action windows' sizing and clipped chips, plus menu polish in the styled
  themes.
