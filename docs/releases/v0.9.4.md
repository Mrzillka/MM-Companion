A big update for the GM's side of the table: minions, squads of NPCs, attacks aimed
straight at a creature on the Scene, and team attacks. It also includes a round of polish
for anyone opening the app for the first time.

**Everyone at an online table needs 0.9.4.** The public server is updated with this
release, and older versions can no longer join a session on it.

## Changes

- **Minions.** Any NPC can be made a minion from its card or sheet. A failed save gives
  it the worst result, its natural 20 is not a critical, and heroes can take 10 against
  it. The GM picks which of these rules apply on the new **Session settings** page.
- **NPC groups.** Quick NPC can make a squad of several at once, and dropping one NPC
  card onto another combines them. A group shows as one framed card with a row per
  member.
- **Aimed attacks.** Right-click any attack and pick a creature off the Scene. The GM
  accepts it, the app judges the hit against the target's defence, and the resulting
  save and conditions land on their own.
- **Team attacks, team checks and group checks** for NPC groups: members combine their
  attacks on one target, help one member's check, or all roll together.
- **End turn rolls recovery.** When the GM ends a creature's turn, Dazed and Stunned now
  get their recovery check: NPCs roll on the server, players get a request to roll.
- **Down and dead creatures fade** on every board. The GM can also tag an NPC as
  summoned.
- **Picking targets is easier.** Area attacks and Impress now pick creatures off a copy
  of the Scene: drag a card up, right-click it or double-click it. The Impress list
  offers only impressions the character can actually make.
- **Ranged reach fixed.** A ranged effect now reaches as far as the book says (Blast 10
  reaches 150 feet, not a mile). Elongation and the Communication and Remote Sensing
  range bands are fixed too.
- **"Fortitude or Will" saves** (Alternate Resistance) now roll the target's better or
  worse bonus instead of +0.
- **Short-Term Removable** costs what the book says: a 20-point power is 16 points
  Removable and 17 Short-Term.
- **Friendlier first run.** File ▸ New and the standard Save and Open shortcuts. The
  Power Constructor asks before closing an unsaved build, and a locked sheet tells you
  how to unlock it (Ctrl+E). The advantage picker is searchable and shows each
  description. Settings has one OK/Cancel/Apply bar, and an empty launcher offers to
  create a character.
- **Tooltips rewritten**: shorter, formatted, and placed on the text they explain.
- Online, another player's roll now waits until your own die has landed. The die no
  longer slides sideways as its result changes.
- Various visual polish and smaller fixes.

**Install:** on Windows, download `MM-Companion-Setup-0.9.4.exe` below and run it; on
Linux, download `MM-Companion-0.9.4-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, extract it and run `./install.sh`.
