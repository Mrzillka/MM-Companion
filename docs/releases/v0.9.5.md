The combat update: every combat maneuver and action from the book is now one click away
under the roller's new **Actions** button. The dice roller itself has been redesigned
around them, and Afflictions and impressions now wear off the way the rules say.

**Everyone at an online table needs 0.9.5.** The public server is updated with this
release, and older versions can no longer join a session on it.

## Changes

- **Combat maneuvers.** All-Out Attack, Accurate Attack, Defensive Attack and Power
  Attack ride on your next attack from a lane under the roller. Set how far to trade,
  and the app keeps you within the book's limits, even with several maneuvers stacked.
- **The Actions menu.** A grid of every combat action: Aim, Charge and Slam, Defend,
  Recover, Drop Prone, Stand, Finishing Attack, Catch, Aid, Feint, Trick, Grab, Trip,
  Disarm, Smash, Escape and Block. Actions against someone else are judged by the GM's
  app, just like an aimed attack.
- **Temporary conditions.** What a maneuver or action leaves behind (All-Out Attack's
  −Defense, Defending's bonus) shows as a red or green condition with its size, plus a
  dot beside each stat it moves. The GM clears them with one button.
- **Team attacks for everyone.** The GM can call a team attack on a target, and any
  player or NPC can join with their next attack.
- **A redesigned dice roller.** Modifier and DC each have one row with quick steps, plus
  Take 10. You can switch to a slider in Settings ▸ General. The roller previews the
  roll before you click. Each history line shows the die, the net bonus and the total,
  and hovering over it shows every bonus and penalty that went in. Natural 20s and 1s
  get a burst, and quick-roll pills are tidier.
- **Afflictions and impressions wear off by the rules.** A condition from an Affliction
  or an impression is shaken off with a new check against its own DC, not the Stamina
  check damage uses. The Affliction's modifiers count: Progressive, Increasing
  Difficulty, Instant Recovery, Cumulative, Concentration, Sustained and more. Recover
  offers this check too.
- **A player's own save now lands.** A save a player rolls off a request or an attack
  card is put on their sheet by the GM's app, the same way an aimed attack's is.
- **Downed creatures get worse on any failed Damage save.** An Incapacitated target who
  fails becomes Dying, and a Dying one becomes Dead, whatever the degree.
- **Prone counts in attacks.** A prone attacker takes −5 on close attacks, and attacks
  against a prone target get +5 close and −5 ranged.
- **Saving against your own attack** off your own card now fills in your numbers and is
  marked with a warning, so it is clear what happened.
- **A loading bar** while windows open, and smooth fades between sheet views.
- **The sheet no longer goes blank** if something fails while it is redrawing, and
  errors are written to a log file in your workspace.
- **Online sessions stay up** after the server restarts its relay. A GM reconnecting
  right after a server restart no longer loses their session.
- The Windows app now carries version information, and there is a privacy policy
  covering the update check and online play.
- Various visual polish and smaller fixes.

**Install:** on Windows, download `MM-Companion-Setup-0.9.5.exe` below and run it; on
Linux, download `MM-Companion-0.9.5-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, extract it and run `./install.sh`.
